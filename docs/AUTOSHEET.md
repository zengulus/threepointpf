# Autosheet coverage and next frontier

## Source boundary

Input: `INGEST THIS - Pathfinder Autosheet (v6.2.1).xlsx`, SHA-256 `f68d978aec205057a4a2e90489505ba395ed9344fbb9da42987b6e0e2e1b6e16`.

`scripts/import-autosheet.mjs` validates with the current local schema, without depending on stale build output. It reads cached values and never evaluates workbook expressions. Generated source-only `.js` bridges support Deno alongside package builds. `--check` compares every expected byte and fails on missing/stale files.

The report inventories all eight sheets (including hidden sheets), imported rows, normalizations/warnings, unsupported regions, and failures. A malformed known layout fails import before replacing output. The workbook is a versioned source snapshot, not an authority for silently resolving PF1e/3.5/third-party differences.

## Authoring and verification

The UI supports N tracks, catalog selection, copying/editing local classes, cumulative charts, automatic class skills with overrides, custom feature operations, equipment and attack profiles. Custom definitions travel with advancement in one snapshot; they cannot shadow imported IDs or aliases. Missing definitions, conflicting effects and invalid charts are errors.

Haste adds at most the intrinsic speed or 30 feet, before movement multipliers. Absent modes stay absent. Armor and medium/heavy carrying loads apply the workbook speed rate in the intrinsic land speed used for that cap. Load-based Dexterity caps and armor check penalties also feed AC and applicable skill checks; load penalties stack with worn armor. Multipliers compose before min/max bounds.

Weapon enhancement participates in typed stacking. Positive Strength multipliers and full negative Strength penalties differ intentionally; off-hand Strength damage uses the applicable half or Double Slice multiplier. Per-weapon full attacks expose BAB iteratives and eligible extras; indices travel through authoritative server recomputation. A resource safeguard rejects sequences over 256 rolls explicitly, without silently truncating them.

The hosted collaborative app uses local accounts and a shared character store. Its demo build still supports a level 1 fighter sample (elite array, Power Attack, Weapon Focus, Toughness, curated greatsword and chain shirt, one fighter advancement slot) and a multi-level showcase sheet; demo mode needs no backend and uses browser storage.

Tests cover aliases/duplicates, cross-track continuation, complete-track aggregation, twelve-track advancement, chart limits, class-skill union, custom effects unlock/undo, lifecycle creation/advancement proposals, HP/skill policy validation and explicit overrides, effect order, all sizes, armor/movement, enhancement stacking, condition composition, overflow, XP boundaries, failed/corrupt persistence, browser/hosted mode resolution and sample derivations, and browser plan parity. ## Exact remaining frontier

1. **HP/skill allocation policy:** the lifecycle profile now turns each derived winning HD and skill-point chassis into an explicit HP-gain and rank-allocation decision, with source provenance, structured policy issues, and a deliberate override where the table permits one. Committed gains/ranks remain ordinary authored character facts; runtime damage is separate. Skill entries and per-level allocations accept fractional values, including half-ranks. The lifecycle wizard offers Average, Average (1st max), Average round up, Average round up (1st max), Maximum and Custom rolled HP policies. Custom rolled HP can be rolled in the wizard using the winning hit die or entered from physical dice; the face and result are retained with the level's HP acquisition. Constitution remains separately applied. The lifecycle wizard now offers Pathfinder and D&D 3.5 class/cross-class rank costs and per-skill caps. Parallel advancement tracks reproduce Gestalt maximum BAB/save totals, per-level maximum hit dice, and the workbook's maximum class-progression skill budget. Retraining and persisted campaign-profile policy remain open.
2. **Contextual combat:** the roll/action-context and roll-outcome contract now exists. A roll names its actor, action, rolled fact and known defense; an action is modeled as a standard attack, a multi-weapon full attack with explicit per-weapon members, or a maneuver; every step carries its own resolvable plan with policy and effective threat range. Implemented scenarios include one shared Haste extra across weapons, touch eligibility (Deadly Aim vs rays), Rapid Shot inside ranged non-touch full attacks, Power Attack handedness, maneuver-conditional CMB and maneuver outcomes, natural-attack counts without BAB iteratives, authored primary/secondary natural-attack roles, the −5 secondary penalty (−2 with the saved Multiattack option), half Strength damage for secondary attacks, and automatic secondary status for natural attacks in a mixed manufactured/natural full attack. Natural attacks now let players specify primary arm, off hand, both, or no arms used; shield occupancy and buckler penalties follow that choice, while unspecified natural attacks remain unrestricted. Other implemented scenarios include sight-based checks, jump checks, partial/standard/soft/improved cover AC and Reflex bonuses, total cover blocking attacks, Improved Precise Shot cover exception for ranged attacks short of total cover, player-entered 1–100% concealment miss checks after an attack beats AC, denied-Dexterity AC, automatic hit/miss, PF1e save naturals, threat ranges, and critical range expansion. The highest applicable concealment chance is entered once; concealment's d100 is displayed as a separate roll and never adds to the attack total; a concealed miss cancels the hit and critical. Damage and initiative use the same contract, including a critical damage variant with the weapon's authored multiplier. The Two-Weapon Fighting penalty table applies to eligible standard attacks and to full attacks that explicitly include a primary and off-hand weapon; light off-hand weapons and the feat are saved character inputs. Off-hand Strength damage is halved unless Double Slice is enabled; Strength penalties remain full. Weapon entries now save proficiency, apply the −4 nonproficiency penalty and retain its source in roll breakdowns. Weapon profiles can define class-level bonus attack progressions; Monk Flurry profiles include the Monk Flurry progression breakpoints in full-attack plans and require a player-authored eligible weapon. Attacks can link another saved character and select that character's normal, touch, flat-footed, or denied-Dexterity AC; target records can be refreshed before a roll. Buckler entries record which hand bears the shield; attacks using that arm apply the −1 attack penalty and remove its shield AC bonus until the player starts a new turn. Ordinary shields now record their arm, block attacks that need that hand (or both hands for two-handed weapons), and lose their shield AC bonus until the next turn when used for a bash. Light and heavy shields can be enabled as authored bash attacks with editable damage dice; tower shields remain unable to bash. Nauseated blocks tracked weapon attacks and spellcasting; Staggered permits one tracked standard or move weapon action and bars tracked full-round attacks; Stunned prevents tracked combat actions and spells. These limits are authored on condition definitions and player-authored features. Stunned drops held items marked in inventory when activated. Line-of-effect geometry, attacks of opportunity against cover and other cover-negating effects, remaining text-only condition restrictions remain open; player-authored system entries now expose and enforce action costs through the shared turn ledger. Combined manufactured/natural full attacks and player-authored Monk Flurry weapon eligibility are supported. The rules follow the [Core Rulebook combat rules](https://legacy.aonprd.com/coreRuleBook/COMBAT.html), [two-weapon penalty and damage rules](https://legacy.aonprd.com/coreRuleBook/COMBAT.html), and [Double Slice feat](https://legacy.aonprd.com/coreRuleBook/feats.html). See `docs/open-decisions.md` for the maneuver, critical-damage, caller-supplied-defense and multi-source extra-attack questions this leaves open.
3. **Equipment composition/inventory:** character-owned identity, currency, item quantities, carried and held states, weights, values, compatible numeric armor/shield material adjustments, the workbook's seven magical storage items, companion inventory and carrying capacity/encumbrance are represented. Companion body type selects the workbook's wondrous-item slot reference and flags assigned codes outside that list. Player worn-item slots follow the workbook's slot list and report items beyond each slot capacity (two rings, one of each other listed slot). Medium/heavy load Dexterity caps, armor check penalties, land-speed rates, and armor/shield nonproficiency penalties affect derived rolls. Armored Kilt attachment is modeled for light and medium armor. Remaining material descriptions, including Dragonhide's “Impervious to Element” and Angelskin's alignment masking, are retained as item notes; Dragonhide does not grant the wearer energy immunity. Equipped armor and shields apply their authored material energy resistance and damage reduction to damage mitigation. Attack entries can author damage dice by size; the engine uses the matching dice for the character's derived size and falls back to the main damage dice for unlisted sizes. The Autosheet does not provide a complete weapon-by-size damage catalog, so entries must author any alternate-size values they need. Other source-specific attachment/slot exceptions, catalog completeness and catalog/composition data for other source-specific containers still need mapping.
4. **Large class systems:** 27 standard casting tables now seed prepared/spontaneous sources, with class-level slots/known progressions, separate workbook class-level and caster-level adjustments, bonus slots, at-will cantrips, caster level, concentration, DCs and sourced spell damage. Nine Psionics and 17 FFd20 profiles import resource capacity, available tiers and known progressions. The workbook provides generic formulas for Spheres, Veilweaving and maneuvers. Character-owned entries now support authored spell school, subschool, descriptors, casting time, components, range, target, area, duration, saves, spell resistance, damage/healing dice, caster-level scaling and per-use resource costs. Custom spells can carry any supported authored rules effects; the author explicitly opts into applying them to the caster, and they contribute only while the spell duration is active. Named spell/power/technique corpora and automated turn rules remain unmodeled. Spell casts use a linked saved target’s relevant saving throw, spell resistance, and touch AC when available, with per-field manual overrides; they resolve negates and half damage and check summed armor/shield spell failure before consuming a cast. Spell entries display component text; custom spell entries can mark somatic components and author partial/disbelief successful-save text, damage, or healing. Those save results now use the authored successful-save effect and suppress the full effect on a successful save; missing text/effects prompt manual resolution. Standard, move, swift/immediate, and full-round spell casting times now consume the shared per-turn action ledger; timed cast periods do not map to combat actions and remain manually tracked. Character-authored spell-like abilities can now execute their linked spell from the Spells tab without using class spell slots, consuming their authored daily use and any authored ability resource costs; spell level, caster level and DC ability are editable, with linked-level, character-level and Charisma defaults. Toggleable abilities and features, plus active system entries, can track player-authored combat rounds; Start new turn decrements those timers and deactivates entries at zero. Spell casts create saved duration entries. Explicit round, minute, and hour durations convert to combat rounds using caster level when the duration says per level; Start new turn decrements and expires them. Unsupported duration wording remains editable as a manual rounds timer. Kineticist attack profiles are imported from Formula References!AD53:AF58 and selectable in the custom attack editor; their blast dice remain character-authored because the workbook supplies no blast dice progression. The workbook records feats and feature names but does not provide general prerequisite or feat-effect rules, so entries without an explicit workbook calculation stay player-authored. Prestige source selection uses lifecycle choices when a progression declares the linked feature requirement.

The roll-context, outcome, damage/initiative plan and check-die contracts are now implemented, including PF1e save naturals, authored critical multipliers and threat-range expansion (Improved Critical/Keen doubling with weapon scoping and nonstacking). Precision damage dice now default to not being multiplied on critical hits, with explicit per-effect override support. A campaign character profile now makes advancement topology, HP/skill allocation, available progression sources, and permitted overrides explicit; it does not settle every PF1e-versus-3.5/3.PF option or combat variant. Runtime LLM interpretation and feat optimization/automation are outside this workbook implementation. Improved Critical itself stays authored per character because the feat must name a weapon, which curated content cannot choose for the player; `appliesWhen.attackIds` is how that choice is recorded.

## Supplement references

Cell/range provenance stays attached to imported content. Supplemental equipment rules cite [Core PRD equipment](https://legacy.aonprd.com/coreRulebook/equipment.html), [Core PRD wondrous items](https://legacy.aonprd.com/corerulebook/magicItems/wondrousItems.html), and [Ultimate Equipment special materials](https://legacy.aonprd.com/ultimateEquipment/armsAndArmor/materials.html); the [Armored Kilt entry](https://aonprd.com/EquipmentArmorDisplay.aspx?ItemName=Armored%20kilt) supplies its attachment rule, and magic-item definitions carry individual PRD links. Handy Haversacks, Bags of Holding, and Portable Holes have fixed carried weights: contents count toward container capacity but do not increase carrying weight. Workbook-dependent variants are marked as Autosheet content rather than universally official rules.

- **Maneuvers tab, rows 23–40:** maneuver and stance names, descriptions, categories, readiness, rolls, and effects belong to each character. The app provides no global maneuver catalog. Each entry has an explicit player-authored Maneuver or Stance type independent of its category label; legacy entries whose category begins with “Stance” remain recognized. Players can add CMD-targeted combat maneuver checks with an authored maneuver slug, optional ability/class-progression overrides, skill checks, weapon strikes, damage or healing rolls, and every character-local effect type supported by the rules engine: typed modifiers, baseline replacement, multiplication, minimum/maximum bounds, granted capabilities, critical-range changes, and additional typed damage dice. A player-authored stance can be activated even with no numeric effect; at most one stance is active across the character's maneuver sources, and using its linked roll does not expend the stance. Unknown entries cannot contribute active effects. Supported conditional effects can be limited by roll kind, attack mode, touch/full-attack status, required or excluded attack tags, maneuver IDs, specific attack IDs, and required or excluded context flags. Entries with an authored action cost spend the matching turn action when activated or used; standard and move actions are unavailable after a full-round action, and swift/immediate actions share one allowance. The player starts a new turn to refresh these allowances. The Charlie example’s entries are editable character data transcribed from the supplied sheet.
- Player-authored system entries expose an action-cost selector for any entry type, including skill checks, damage/healing rolls, CMD checks, and custom resource abilities. Their use spends the selected standard, move, swift, immediate, full-round, or free action through the shared turn ledger and respects tracked condition limits. Skill-check entries also honor authored per-day uses and resource costs. Activated non-stance maneuvers expend on use; active stances stay available for repeat checks unless the player sets a use limit. Weapon-attack maneuvers let the player author a strike count (1–20); each strike independently resolves against the selected target and rolls its own hit, critical, and applicable damage. Player-authored entry effects apply during that entry’s weapon, skill, CMD, and standalone damage rolls; standalone damage selects melee or ranged applicability and preserves typed added dice and excluded-effect provenance. Healing keeps its authored flat healing bonus separate from HP-capacity effects. Maneuver-specific conditions use the authored maneuver slug.
